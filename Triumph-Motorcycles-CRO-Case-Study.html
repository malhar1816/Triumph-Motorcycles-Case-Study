Triumph Motorcycles Digital Marketing Case Study

How we increased configurator completions by 25% and test ride requests by 18% through strategic UX optimization and digital marketing excellence.

Executive Summary

This case study explores Triumph Motorcycles' conversion rate optimization strategy to enhance user journeys and boost conversions on triumphmotorcycles.co.uk, with a focus on model pages, test ride requests, and configurator engagement, aligned with global marketing objectives.

25% Increase

In configurator completions, exceeding the initial target of 20% within six months of implementation.

18% Growth

In test ride requests, surpassing the 15% objective and closing the gap with industry benchmarks.

10% Enhancement

In overall user engagement metrics, demonstrating improved customer experience across all touchpoints.

Background & Challenge

Triumph Motorcycles, a premium motorcycle leader, faced significant challenges with their digital presence that needed strategic intervention.

The Brand

Triumph, established as a premium motorcycle leader, uses triumphmotorcycles.co.uk to engage B2C customers worldwide with iconic models like the Speed Twin 1200. The brand emphasizes experiential marketing and customer loyalty.

The Problem

  • 45% configurator drop-off at the accessory selection stage

  • 35% lower test ride conversions (5% vs. 7% industry average)

  • Inconsistent user journeys across global markets

  • Unoptimized content affecting sales potential

Strategy & Implementation

Our comprehensive approach combined analytics, testing, and CMS updates to transform Triumph's digital experience.

Analytics & Insights

Google Analytics Implementation

Pinpointed 45% configurator drop-offs and identified key user flow bottlenecks through enhanced tracking and event monitoring.

Hotjar Heatmaps & Recordings

Revealed CTAs positioned below mobile fold and identified user hesitation points in the configurator journey.

Optimizely Testing Framework

Established robust metrics tracking and created a systematic approach to multivariate testing across key conversion points.

A/B Testing Strategy

Configurator Simplification

Tested streamlined configurator interfaces with progress indicators and simplified accessory selection, resulting in 15% completion improvement.

CTA Optimization

Repositioned CTAs to top sections of model pages, resulting in 18% increase in click-through rates and improved mobile engagement.

Regional Personalization

Implemented market-specific accessories (e.g., UK heated grips, US touring packages), lifting regional engagement by 10%.

CMS Updates

Adobe Experience Manager Implementation

  • Updated Tiger 1200 pages globally with consistent CTAs and messaging

  • Synchronized content with ongoing marketing campaigns across regions

  • Implemented seasonal promotions while maintaining brand tone and voice

  • Streamlined asset management for consistent brand presentation

Project Management

Asana Workflow Implementation

  • Coordinated content, design, and regional teams effectively

  • Met Tiger 1200 launch deadlines across all markets simultaneously

  • Established clear approval workflows for regional content adaptations

  • Created transparent reporting dashboards for stakeholder updates

UX Optimization

Strategic improvements to the user experience drove significant conversion gains across key touchpoints.

Homepage Redesign

  • • Simplified navigation hierarchy
  • • Persistent CTAs across viewport
  • • Mobile-optimized layout
  • • Featured models carousel
  • • Prominent dealer locator

Configurator Enhancement

  • • Step-by-step wizard interface
  • • Clear progress indicator
  • • Save & resume functionality
  • • 360° model viewer integration
  • • Real-time price updates

Test Ride Journey

  • • Simplified form (5 fields vs 8)
  • • Dealer proximity integration
  • • Available model inventory
  • • Calendar scheduling tool
  • • SMS confirmation system

Before & After: Configurator Experience

Before Optimization

  • All accessories shown at once (overwhelming)

  • No progress indicator or save functionality

  • Limited motorcycle visualization angles

  • Delayed price updates on selection

After Optimization

  • Categorized accessory selection with visual previews

  • Clear 5-step progress indicator with save option

  • Interactive 360° model with zoom capabilities

  • Real-time price updates and finance calculator
